Dance Styles Offered

Find Your Rhythm

From classic Texas Honky-Tonk Two-Step to high-energy Country Swing, we teach dynamic styles for every dancer.

Texas Two-Step

The king of Texas dance halls! Counter-clockwise progressive dance characterized by smooth quick-quick-slow-slow rhythm and elegant turns.

Difficulty: Beginner Friendly
Country Swing

High-energy, fast-paced, and full of excitement! Features spins, dips, pretzel arms, and flashy moves tailored to fast country songs.

Energy: High / Dynamic
Line Dancing

No partner needed! Learn popular floor fillers like the Copperhead Road, Shivers, Watermelon Crawl, and modern trending line dances.

Partner Required: None
West Coast Swing

Smooth, elastic, and versatile slotted swing style danced to contemporary country, blues, and pop beats. Great for improvisational flair!

Vibe: Smooth & Creative

Why Join Aggie Country Dance Club?

Whether you want to learn to dance for Texas A&M Traditions, prepare for weddings and dance halls, or just make awesome lifelong friends, ACDC is your home on campus!

No Experience or Partner Needed

We rotate partners constantly throughout lessons. You'll meet dozens of new people every Monday!

Affordable Dues

Your first two Monday lessons are completely FREE! Semester dues are minimal and cover club socials and t-shirts.

Welcoming Community

A friendly, supportive environment where everyone encourages each other regardless of skill level.
